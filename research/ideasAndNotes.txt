                Implementation Notes Decisions
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Integration of very dense format in datastructure doesn't make
    any sense. The adding of a vertex needs a removal of an 
    element in (possible the middle of) the list. This arises
    too much bookkeeping.
- The efficient counting of the bits in bitarray is done with 
    __builtin_popcount(). This is compiler specific, but proven
    to be fast. A compiler independent variant could be taken 
    from "Hackers Delight" (Chapter 5)
